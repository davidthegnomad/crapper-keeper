Crapper Keeper Privacy Policy

Effective July 17, 2026

Crapper Keeper is a notes application operated by Gnomad Studio. This policy covers the web, iOS, Android, and browser-extension versions.

Information we process

  • Account identifiers supplied by Google or Apple, such as your user ID, email address, display name, and profile image.
  • Content you create or save, including notebooks, chapters, notes, links, selected text, and uploaded images.
  • Basic service data needed by Firebase to authenticate requests, protect accounts, and operate cloud storage.

How information is used

Your information is used only to authenticate you, synchronize your notes across devices, store requested uploads, and operate Crapper Keeper. The app does not sell personal information and does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

Storage and service providers

Authentication and app data are processed using Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage. Google and Apple process sign-in data under their own privacy policies.

Deletion

You can delete your account and associated Crapper Keeper cloud data inside the app: open Account Settings and choose Delete account and data. A recent sign-in is required for security.

Children

Crapper Keeper is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@gnomadstudio.org
